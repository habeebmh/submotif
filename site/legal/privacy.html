Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026. Release packaging and downloads remain on hold pending review.

SUBMOTIF by Habeeb is maintained by Habeeb. This policy describes the project's own software and website. Your DAW, operating system, instrument plugins, GitHub, and any social platform you visit have separate privacy practices.

The plugin and Max for Live device

The current SUBMOTIF code generates patterns on your computer. It contains no account system, advertising, analytics, automatic crash-report upload, or automatic update checker. It does not send your MIDI, audio, project files, or settings to the maintainer. Your DAW can save device settings and patterns in a session, and MIDI export writes a file locally. Host applications and third-party instruments may independently process data under their own policies.

The website

The page has no advertising trackers, analytics SDK, registration form, mailing list, or payment form. Fonts, graphics, and preview audio are served as local site assets. The browser generator computes notes and synthesizes its preview tone in your browser. Downloading MIDI saves a local file; the generated pattern is not uploaded.

The audition gallery stores your selected candidate IDs in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload. This is functional storage, not a tracking identifier. Picks are not sent to the maintainer automatically. Use Clear picks, or clear this site's browser data, to remove them. Copying or downloading a selection summary is your choice; you decide whether and where to share it.

Hosting and external links

The website uses Firebase Hosting. When a hosted page is requested, the hosting provider necessarily processes network/request information, which can include an IP address, browser information, requested URLs, and request times, to deliver and protect the service. Provider-operated logs and retention are governed by the provider's terms; the project does not claim that the host keeps no logs. See Firebase privacy and security information.

Source code, issues, and pull requests are hosted on GitHub. Future official downloads will use GitHub Releases after review. Visiting GitHub links or downloading a published release contacts GitHub and is subject to GitHub's privacy statement. A social link opens that platform only when you choose it; the page contains no embedded social tracking widget. External providers may process data internationally under their own arrangements.

Information you choose to share

If you submit an issue or contribution, the maintainer can see the information you provide, such as your GitHub username, comment, attachment, and commit metadata. Public submissions are visible to others and may remain in repository history, forks, or archives. That information is used to respond, maintain the project, review contributions, and document changes. Do not include private recordings, secrets, or personal information in a public report.

The project does not sell personal information or use it for behavioral advertising. There is no separate visitor-profile database. Public project history is generally retained for maintenance and provenance; provider logs are managed by the providers. The project cannot promise to remove copies held in independent forks or archives.

Your choices and requests

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information. For a project-related request, contact Habeeb through the GitHub profile, or ask for a private contact method in a minimal public project issue. Do not post the sensitive details of your request publicly. For information controlled by GitHub or Google, use that provider's privacy-request channels. This policy does not limit rights available under applicable law.

Changes

The date at the top changes when this policy is revised. New data collection features require a corresponding policy update before they are introduced.
