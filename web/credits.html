Credits

Submotif’s product interface and engine are by Habeeb. The site shows the real native editor, the six owner-approved MIDI phrases and waveforms measured from their recordings. The graphics are generated from those files; the source and provenance are included in the repository.

Audio examples

The six examples use the approved Submotif MIDI selections. The Acid recording uses Serum 2 with the owner’s selected acid preset, transposed two octaves higher as approved. No Serum plugin or preset is included in the project or downloads.
